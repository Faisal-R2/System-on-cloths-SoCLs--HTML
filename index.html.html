Cloud Manufacturing for Embroidered Electronics

System on Clothing (SoCl)

Designing the cloud manufacturing network for the WearLab.

Bridging the gap between Electronic Design Automation (EDA) and Cloud Manufacturing to create durable, washable, and comfortable embroidered electronic textiles.

The Rigid-Flexible Barrier

Traditional wearables rely on rigid PCBs that cause skin irritation. We replace rigid packages with flexible, conductive embroidery directly integrated into the textile architecture.

Cloud Manufacturing (MaaS)

By connecting individual designers to a distributed manufacturing cloud, we optimize resource allocation, providing on-demand fabrication of medical-grade smart garments.

Sensors and Electrodes

Developing embroidered electrodes and sensors to measure vital signals (EMG, ECG, EEG, force, temperature). These enable flexible, comfortable, and continuous health-monitoring.

Algorithmic Toolpaths

Our custom EDA platform mathematically synthesizes geometries for passive sensors (resistors, capacitors) and explicitly manages machine states to guarantee electrical isolation.

Fabrication Materials Library

HC-12 Thread Conductive

A highly conductive multi-filament thread optimized for low-resistance signal routing and power delivery across the textile substrate.

Resistance: ~X Ω/m
Ideal for: Main traces, Antennas

HC-40 Thread Conductive

A finer conductive thread suitable for high-density routing, detailed sensor geometries, and intricate electrode patterns.

Resistance: ~Y Ω/m
Ideal for: Fine-pitch components, tight radii

Silver-Coated Nylon Biocompatible

Combines the flexibility of nylon with the high conductivity and antibacterial properties of silver. Perfect for skin-contact electrodes.

Properties: Antibacterial, Washable
Ideal for: ECG/EMG pads, Sweat sensors

Resistive Yarn Passive

Carbon-infused or specialized alloy threads used to physically embroider passive resistors directly into the garment.

Resistivity (ρ): ~Z Ω/m
Ideal for: Custom embroidered resistors, Heaters
